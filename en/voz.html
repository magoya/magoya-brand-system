Personality

Strategic, accountable,
curious, candid.

The order matters: each trait balances the next. We don’t talk to show how much we know, but to make better decisions together.

OrderTraitIn one lineWhat it balances
01StrategicSees what’s behind the obvious problem.The core trait. Lets us be curious and candid without being confrontational.
02AccountableOwns what it proposes.Regulates candor: “in our view” instead of “this is completely wrong.”
03CuriousAsks to open up possibilities.Carries the challenger edge: curiosity that looks for variables to enrich the strategy.
04CandidSays what needs saying.Comes in once there’s trust. Never combative.
MandatoryAlways “accountable,” never “responsible.” Accountable means owning the outcome.

What we build first

Three layers, in order.

A brand is built over the long term, with patience and repetition. We work a few messages at a time, and move to the next set once these hold.

01
“Hi, I’m Magoya.”

A hands-on software and AI consulting firm for agribusiness. Without this base, no other message has anything to stand on.

02
“I’m the usage expert.”

No consulting firm is associated with usage the way we propose it. Owning that territory gives us a differentiating angle.

03
“I’m a reference in the market.”

A thought-leader brand. The priority once the first two layers are built.

Brand-building goals by market

Bigger than they think.

Cross-market goalMagoya is understood as a hands-on software and AI consulting firm for agribusiness, with the scale, experience, and reach to work with the sector’s leaders.
MarketWhere we startWhat communication pursues

We lead with the end result:

50%

30%

Our stance

We stand for understanding, doing,
and staying with it.

StanceWhat it means
Part of the ecosystemWe don’t come in to say everything is wrong. We’re part of the problem and we propose the solution.
Respect for what’s been achievedThe client got this far. Before building something new, we ask what can evolve before starting over.
We work from where the client isWhat already exists is the starting point, not the enemy.
Fact-based, not intuitiveWhat we suggest rests on evidence and measurable data. We show every option, even the ones that aren’t viable yet.
Launch is only the beginningThis stance underpins the usage territory.

What we stand against

We show the work
behind every answer.

Our opposite is the black-box approach: the belief that users don’t care how things get solved. We’re for showing, proving, explaining, and educating. These twelve are starting points for brand points of view.

01Measuring success at launch instead of in usage.
02Proposing the same solution for everyone.
03Delivering a product and walking away from its implementation.
04Building without the conviction that it will be useful.
05“I already know how to fix it” before understanding the problem.
06Reinventing the wheel, or saying nothing the client has is any good.
07Overpromising and underdelivering.
08Teams that don’t measure. Consultants who don’t do.
09Blaming the user when the software doesn’t fit them.
10Arrogance: “I already know everything.”
11Falling in love with a product that doesn’t solve a real problem.
12Starting from zero when what exists can be reworked.

What Magoya never says · Mandatory

Banned phrases.

Never sayWhy
“What you have doesn’t work.” · “We need to redo everything.”Contradicts our respect for what’s been achieved and our rule of building on what exists.
“Only we can solve this.”That’s arrogance. Magoya doesn’t compete by putting others down.
“This is how it’s done.”We don’t believe in one truth without context. The brand has no standard recipes.
“We transform.”“Transform” stays internal so we don’t overpromise impact. It also drags in “digital transformation,” which is worn out.
“This technology is easy to apply.” · “These problems are easy to solve.”Quick-fix thinking. Easy answers shrink our own work and sound like someone who doesn’t know agriculture.
“The problem is the client’s” or “the user’s.”Magoya doesn’t point fingers. It proposes the solution.

Words we don’t use to describe ourselves: Language. How every claim is backed: Evidence rules.
