Same idea, two spokespeople

What we saw → where the problem was → how we worked on it

Cami · Usage · Field audience

“Recently, with a client, we saw certain types of users drop a monitoring app in week three. The problem wasn’t the app, it was adoption: once the initial curiosity wore off, the app didn’t fit into that user’s real routine in the field.

We worked on adoption: guided onboarding for the first few weeks, and usage metrics by stage to spot exactly when usage drops.”

Pato · Integration · CTOs

“We saw an AI recommendation model that worked well in testing and failed in production. The problem was in the data: sensors reported at different frequencies, and nobody had defined what to do when a data point arrived late.

We worked on the architecture: we defined the data contract between systems before touching the model again.”

Content focus and production

What we publish, and how

Five content focuses
  1. The Magoya Method, stage by stage. We’ve never told it, and it sets us apart.
  2. Brand points of view for each territory.
  3. AI from the builder’s side: what works and what fails in deployments. (The user’s side is IA en Campo.)
  4. Cases without logos: aggregated lessons, with validated numbers only.
  5. Presence in each country: meetings, trips, field visits.
How a piece gets made
  1. Set the topic; give the spokesperson a title or statement.
  2. They send a voice note, a draft, or do a 30-minute interview.
  3. We curate and refine, and ask for a real example.
  4. The spokesperson validates. It stays in their voice.

Cadence: at least one article per spokesperson per month (goal: two). Mix: 90% expert, 10% human. Not Magoya content: field tips, tool tutorials, farmer education. That’s IA en Campo.

Evidence rules

Every claim can be traced

Start from evidence

What we observed, did, measured, or reasoned. Hypotheses and trends are fine when labeled.

Telling a case

What we saw → where the problem was → what we did → the result. No approved measurement: only confirmed outcomes.

Generalizations

No hunch as industry fact. State the scope. Never invent project counts, timeframes, or percentages.

Figures

Validated by someone at Magoya, with unit, scope, period, and baseline. AI never invents or fills in numbers.

Clients

Not named or identifiable, even by elimination, without explicit authorization.

Capabilities

Say what capability is gained and what value it creates. Never reduce it to headcount cuts.
