Who we are · What we do

We’re a hands-on software and AI
consulting firm for agribusiness.

Who we are

We understand the industry, the business, the software, and the context each product has to work in. We look at the whole picture: we start from what’s already there, and we can execute what we recommend.

We come in at any stage, from strategy through design and development to use in the field.

We work with a method built to deliver results. Every step has a hypothesis, a plan, and a success KPI.

What we do

We work with agribusinesses to solve their business challenges with software, AI, and agricultural expertise. We don’t stop at advice: we build, implement, and stay until it’s used.

How we come in. We come in to understand the problem, find the opportunities, and define what’s worth solving, how, and when. We design and develop solutions, integrate software, data, and ecosystems, and support implementation, adoption, usage, and evolution.

We partner for the long term. From strategy to execution, we work in partnership with our clients so their software and AI work, are valued by the people who use them, evolve with the business, and support its growth.

Value proposition

One team, one method, the whole cycle

Magoya works with agribusinesses to solve their business challenges with software and AI. We work hands-on across the three spaces where problems live: strategy, design and development, and adoption and usage.

StrategyWe answer the questions that come before the product: whether there’s product-market fit, whether the business model is viable, what to add to a platform to reach a new target or country, or where AI fits into the company.
Design and developmentWe design, build, and implement software and AI, whether it’s a new product or an existing one that needs to improve, integrate, or scale.
Adoption and usageWe work with teams in the field and in their daily operations: onboarding, adoption, support at every level, and measuring usage and the value it creates.

The three spaces · Mandatory names

We bring together what usually sits with three different vendors.

Strategy, design and development, and adoption and usage are usually handled by three companies that don’t talk to each other. At Magoya they sit together in one team, under one method, so we can build connected solutions across all three spaces and start wherever the client’s pain is strongest.

Territory 1 · Usage · 50%

Software has no value in itself.
Its value is in its use.

A software product succeeds when it gets used and delivers value to the user.

For Magoya, usage isn’t a stage: it’s the north star for the whole product cycle. From ideation and design through implementation, onboarding, adoption, and evolution, the questions are always the same: who’s going to use this, in what context, and what value should it create for them?

Adoption is the moment someone goes from non-user to user. It’s part of usage, not a synonym for it. Sustained usage doesn’t happen on its own: it happens when the product creates value for the person using it.

The Magoya Method

No execution without Discovery

StageWhat happensWhat the client gets
1 · DiscoveryWe immerse ourselves in the problem with stakeholders and users, and analyze the available materials and data.A shared understanding of the problem and prioritized findings agreed with stakeholders. Closes with a workshop.
2 · IdeationWe build solution options together with the client.Solution alternatives with their trade-offs and a weighted recommendation. Closes with a selection workshop.
3 · PlanningWe build an execution plan and a measurement strategy, with clear hypotheses and KPIs.An agreed plan with resources, roadmap, deliverables, and expected metrics.
4 · ExecutionWe implement iteratively, with visible progress and adjustments along the way.Results validated in short cycles.
5 · Measurement and evolutionWe measure against the agreed KPIs, and what we learn feeds the next cycle.Evidence of impact and a clear decision: scale, adjust, or change course.

The tagline

Software and AI
for agribusiness.

Always in English, in every language.

The tagline’s direction · Functional now, inspirational later

The brand was built by doing, not by saying, so it’s at an earlier stage than its years suggest. The name Magoya carries no built-in association, so the tagline does the positioning work. An emotional tagline comes once awareness is where we want it.

What it can drop

“Consulting firm” and “hands-on.” They come right after, in the signature or the introduction. The tagline doesn’t have to say everything.

The qualifier

Something beyond the build that points to usage or value. “Usage” is more specific and differentiating than “results,” which fits any consulting firm.

Non-negotiable base

Software + AI + agribusiness. Without it the brand is too ambiguous and loses its niche.
