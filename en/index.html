How to use this edition

Same meaning in both languages. Native wording in each.

Source hierarchy

The Spanish playbook is the source of truth for strategy, positioning, offer, and method. This edition sets how it’s said in English. Facts about projects come only from approved team sources.

Alignment rule

English is written natively, never translated line by line. Any future change is recorded in the separate alignment log, with the Spanish line and the English line, and needs team approval.

Labels

  • Principle: how we think.
  • Recommendation: good practice; use judgment.
  • Mandatory: no exceptions without written approval.
