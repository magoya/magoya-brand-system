Working with AI

The rules any AI agent follows when it writes for Magoya, from the English edition of the Magoya Brand Voice Playbook.

Instructions for AI agents

Before writing, and before delivering

Instructions

  1. Identify goal, audience, channel, language, source, and confidentiality limits. Missing something essential? Ask.
  2. Don’t invent facts, figures, results, quotes, clients, dates, or experiences.
  3. Separate fact, interpretation, hypothesis, and example.
  4. Spanish input: rebuild it for an English reader, via the glossary.
  5. Conflicting sources or rules: flag it, don’t resolve it silently.
  6. Decisions, risks, or material figures: deliver as a draft for review.
  7. Confirm changing numbers are current.

Checklist before delivering

  • Answers the brief; respects the positioning.
  • Constructive, not arrogant, with a useful view.
  • Every real figure has a human validation.
  • Usage, adoption, the three spaces, and the method used correctly.
  • No banned words; “AI” and “IA en Campo” correct.
  • Native English: active voice, US spelling, serial comma, no jargon.
  • Anything pending is flagged.
Source: Magoya Brand Voice Playbook, English edition · October 2026 · p. 62
